# Sample assignment

FICTION. Camera demo only. Do not publish as history or as a true story.

Prop: a single knife-notch in the heel of a loaf

The town taxed bread by the cut slice. A notch was how a loaf admitted it had been opened. A second notch dried a different color, and that color was the lie.

Total 1765 frames, 58.8s at 30 fps. Sets: bakery-dawn, market-stall, guild-table.
