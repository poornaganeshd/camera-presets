# 2.5D Deadpan & Uncanny Cinema — Camera Rig & Preset Specification

## 1. Architectural Overview
This specification defines a deterministic, frame-accurate camera rig and preset system for a **Three.js + Remotion** (60fps) programmatic video pipeline. The visual grammar relies on rigid geometry, uncomfortable stillness, abrupt mechanical transitions, and 2.5D diorama depth.

### Two-Node Rig Hierarchy
Never animate the `THREE.PerspectiveCamera` directly in world space. All transforms are isolated into a two-node hierarchy to prevent gimbal lock and allow independent composition offsets:
1. **`CameraTrackNode` (`THREE.Group`):** Handles macro world translation (`X, Y, Z`) and base target look-at coordinates.
2. **`CameraBodyNode` (`THREE.PerspectiveCamera`):** Child of `CameraTrackNode`. Handles focal length (`fov`), Dutch angle (`rotation.z`), micro-jitter, and frustum compensation.

## 2. 5-Plane Z-Depth Bucket Specification
To achieve a paper-cutout diorama look without perspective distortion breaking the layout when changing Z-depth, assets are locked to 5 discrete Z-planes. 

* **Reference Plane ($Z_{ref}$):** `0` (Camera default rests at $Z_{cam} = 10$, distance $D_{ref} = 10$).
* **Frustum Scale Compensation Formula:** When placing a 2D cut-out plane at depth $Z_{layer}$, scale its mesh by $S$ so its apparent screen size matches the reference plane prior to camera movement:
  $$S = \frac{Z_{cam} - Z_{layer}}{Z_{cam} - Z_{ref}}$$

| Bucket ID | Z-Coordinate | Scale Factor ($S$) | Overscan Margin | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `BG_SKY` | -15.0 | 2.50x | +40% | Static backdrop, sky gradients, distant textures |
| `BG_ENV` | -6.0 | 1.60x | +25% | Distant buildings, suburban horizon, silhouettes |
| `MID_STAGE` | 0.0 | 1.00x | +10% | Primary action plane, main characters, hero props |
| `FG_PROP` | +3.0 | 0.70x | +15% | Foreground framing elements (fences, desks, bushes) |
| `FG_VIGNETTE` | +6.0 | 0.40x | +20% | Out-of-focus silhouette framing, pillarboxes |

## 3. Core Camera Presets (The Visual Grammar)

### Preset 1: `deadpan-lock`
* **Intent:** Uncomfortable, bureaucratic stillness. The frame refuses to move while absurd or mundane action plays out in the center.
* **FOV:** `35°` (flat, telephoto compression).
* **Motion:** Zero translation. Optional stepped micro-breathing (`0.015` units on Y-axis quantized to 12fps to mimic a locked tripod on a wooden floor).

### Preset 2: `tension-push`
* **Intent:** Slow, creeping paranoia. Imperceptible at first, locking onto a subject's face or a mundane object.
* **FOV:** `40°` locked.
* **Motion:** Linear Z-axis translation from `Z = 10` to `Z = 6.5` over the full shot duration. No easing in or out (`easeLinear`)—starts moving on frame 0 and cuts abruptly while still moving.

### Preset 3: `parallax-slide`
* **Intent:** Architectural inspection. The camera glides laterally on a rigid dolly track, exposing depth between the 5 Z-planes.
* **FOV:** `45°`.
* **Motion:** Pure X-axis translation (`-2.5` to `+2.5`) with `CameraTrackNode` looking strictly parallel (`lookAt(currentX, currentY, 0)`). Eased with a heavy mechanical curve (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1.0)`).

### Preset 4: `whip-pan-snap`
* **Intent:** Comedic reveal or sudden shift to a secondary character reacting in deadpan silence.
* **FOV:** `45°`.
* **Motion:** Holds static until `triggerFrame`, then translates X by `±8.0` units over exactly `8 frames` (at 60fps = `0.133s`) using an aggressive exponential curve (`cubic-bezier(0.85, 0, 0.15, 1)`), followed by a `3-frame` damped overshoot settle.

### Preset 5: `vertigo-creep`
* **Intent:** Sudden psychological realization or uncanny dread without cutting away.
* **Motion:** Simultaneous dolly-out (`Z` moves from `7.0` to `14.0`) while zooming in (`FOV` narrows from `55°` down to `28°`).
* **Math:** Keep the `MID_STAGE` (`Z = 0`) subject width constant:
  $$FOV(t) = 2 \cdot \arctan\left(\frac{W}{2 \cdot Z_{cam}(t)}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{180}{\pi}\right)$$
  where $W$ is the locked frustum width at frame 0:
  $$W = 2 \cdot Z_{cam}(0) \cdot \tan\left(\frac{FOV(0) \cdot \pi}{360}\right)$$

### Preset 6: `pedestal-reveal`
* **Intent:** Rigid vertical elevator scan (bottom-to-top or top-to-bottom) revealing an absurdly tall prop or subterranean layer.
* **FOV:** `38°`.
* **Motion:** Pure Y-axis translation (`Y = -4.0` to `Y = 0.0`) with zero pitch tilt.
